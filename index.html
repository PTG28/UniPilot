U Uni Students + Smart Advisor

Pick the right courses. Pass the ones you missed.

An extension for the Uni Students app, which already shows the courses you passed and failed each semester. Smart Advisor turns that transcript into guidance.

  • Recommender: best courses for next semester, ranked by fit with your grades, prerequisites and interests
  • Predict: your likely grade in each recommended course
  • Predict: your chance of passing each failed course, and how a focus plan raises it
  • Focus: most frequent exam topics + tips from students who passed

Clickable mockup, all data simulated. Try: open Next Semester and build a 30-ECTS plan, then open Retake Coach → Statistics and tick items in the focus plan.
