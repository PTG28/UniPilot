U Uni Students + Smart Advisor

Choose the right courses. Pass your exams.

An extension for the existing Uni Students app. Uni Students shows your grades; Smart Advisor uses them to guide you.

  • Choose Courses (recommender): which courses to take next semester, ranked by fit with your grades, prerequisites and interests
  • Exam Coach (predictive): your chance of passing, the most frequent exam topics and tips from students who passed
  • AI Revision (predictive): an AI mock test that predicts how ready you are and your likely grade before the exam
