S StudySync

Know if you'll be ready before the exam.

StudySync is a study companion for university students. A predictive model forecasts your readiness for every exam from the sessions you log, and a recommender tells you what to study next and at which hour you'll focus best.

  • Predict: readiness on exam day, with a confidence band
  • Predict: your personal focus level hour by hour
  • Recommend: the next best topic + time slot
  • Recommend: an auto-generated weekly plan

This is a clickable mockup — all data is simulated. Try: start the recommended session, skip to the end, rate it, and watch the predictions update.
